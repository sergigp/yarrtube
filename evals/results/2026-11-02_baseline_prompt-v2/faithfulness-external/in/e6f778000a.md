The video explains cross-entropy, a concept from information theory that connects compression and language model training. It begins with a 2002 paper that used file compression to discover language relationships without pre-existing linguistic knowledge, then defines cross-entropy as a measure of how well a compression scheme optimized for one context performs in another. The video shows that cross-entropy is the loss function used in pre-training and distilling language models, where minimizing it trains the model to compress text efficiently. It concludes that this approach demonstrates "compression is intelligence" by training language models to be optimal compressors.
