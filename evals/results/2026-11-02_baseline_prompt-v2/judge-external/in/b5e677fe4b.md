Summary:
El video analiza la problemática del cambio climático y la dificultad de implementar soluciones efectivas bajo el modelo capitalista actual. Se examina el Acuerdo de París, criticando su enfoque en el crecimiento verde y la confianza en la tecnología para desacoplar el crecimiento económico de la destrucción ambiental, señalando que este enfoque no cuestiona los patrones de consumo ni la explotación de recursos. Se exploran alternativas como el “Green New Deal” y el decrecimiento, destacando la necesidad de un cambio fundamental en la organización social y económica, priorizando la sostenibilidad y la justicia social sobre el crecimiento ilimitado. Finalmente, el video concluye que la cuestión climática se reduce a una disputa ideológica sobre qué tipo de futuro se desea construir, enfatizando la importancia de repensar nuestras prioridades y adoptar modelos de vida más sostenibles.

- id: paris-agreement-goals-and-weaknesses
  fact: The Paris Agreement (COP21) aimed to keep warming well below 2 °C, aspiring to 1.5 °C; it was a diplomatic success but its commitments are non-binding with no real sanctions.
- id: climate-policy-adapts-capitalism
  fact: Following philosopher Kohei Saito, the video argues that Paris and later policies (Green New Deal, Fit for 55, REPowerEU, Inflation Reduction Act) do not question the economic system but try to adapt capitalism via green growth and technology, a techno-optimism it considers insufficient.
  common mistake: The video presents green growth / the Green New Deal as the solution to climate change.
  note: Captions spell Saito's first name 'Coge'; correct name is Kohei Saito.
- id: decoupling-would-come-too-late
  fact: Absolute decoupling of growth from emissions might take around 100 years, too late because cuts must happen in the next 10–20 years.
  common mistake: Decoupling can be achieved within the next 10–20 years.
- id: north-externalizes-costs-to-south
  fact: Rich Global North countries keep their consumption by externalizing ecological and social costs to the Global South ('imperial mode of living'), and green policies reproduce this logic.
  note: Captions write 'Ulrick Brand y Marcus Bisen'; correct names are Ulrich Brand and Markus Wissen.
- id: conclusion-embrace-degrowth
  fact: The video concludes that a future on Earth requires alternative ways of life that embrace degrowth, since Western consumption levels cannot be universalized; the ecological transition is a dispute between ideas of the future, not a technical question.
  common mistake: The video concludes capitalism can be fixed with small adjustments or green technology.
